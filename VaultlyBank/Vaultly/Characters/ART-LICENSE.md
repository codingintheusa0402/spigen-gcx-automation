- **`ch_<item>_…png` (body, heads, hair, clothes, accessories):** from the [Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator](https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator) by the Liberated Pixel Cup artists. Used under **CC-BY-SA 3.0 / OGA-BY 3.0** (CC-BY / CC0 where offered). We never use GPL-only art. Recoloured or modified versions (e.g. the white doctor's coat made from the gray trench) are released under the same licence, as share-alike requires. Every source file, its authors and licences are in `credits.json`, and they're shown in the app under 설정 → 픽셀 아트 크레딧.
- **`ch_custom_*` and `ch_prop_*`:** original Vaultly art drawn from scratch (delivery helmet, stethoscope, badge, scooter, desk, counter, box, bowl, cup ramen, bed, Zzz). Only head positions were measured from LPC sheets to place the helmet.

Rebuild with `python3 scripts/build_characters.py --lpc <LPC clone>`.
